    Story & Lore

    The isle ships a 12-quest main story chain and 13 unlockable lore fragments. Both live on their own tabs; quests are on the isle Quests tab, fragments are in the Lore Master (from the isle Home tab).

    How the chain works

    • Sequential. Quest N is locked until quest N-1 completes. You cannot skip ahead by finishing later objectives first.
    • Auto-completion. A quest completes the moment its predicate is met. There is no "claim" step; the reward and the next quest unlock immediately. Consuming the materials afterwards does not un-complete a quest.
    • Progress persists. Once a quest is stamped complete, mainland activity, save-slot switches, and even resetting your inventory of the quest's target material do nothing to it.
    • Rewards are narrative, not stat. The reward on each quest is a lore fragment, an act unlock, or (for the final quest) the Ancient Signet plus the Isle Champion title.

    Objectives that reference dungeon counts (Beach & Cliffs, Ancient Forest, Volcano Peak, Abyssal Depths) accept any completed clear on that dungeon, from any slot, at any level. Objectives that reference an item count read your current shared inventory.

    Lore fragments

    Fragments live in the Lore Master on the isle Home tab. They unlock as story quests complete; each act contributes one prologue-style fragment plus three per act, for 13 total. Fragments are pure prose (no rewards), meant to fill in the isle's backstory: what the Elders were, why the Ritual exists, and why Rowan wanted you along.

    • Prologue: Rowan's Note.
    • Act I (three fragments): the coastline's kept past, the memory in the rock, and the ancient fish.
    • Act II (three): the grove as a garden, the ritual against dying, and the order that made it.
    • Act III (three): the peak as their altar, the seal's alloy, and the one who refused to sit down.
    • Act IV (three): the flooding chamber, the ritual's true cost, and the epilogue of what you carry home.

    The full text is in-app; the wiki holds only titles.

    Isle Champion title

    Defeating the Last Elder the first time unlocks the Isle Champion title, listed alongside every other title in Titles. Ironman does not gate it.
